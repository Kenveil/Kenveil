<h3 align="center">A privacy app for Windows that shows how your own accounts connect,<br>and helps you close the paths between them.</h3>

---

### 👋 About

Kenveil is built and run by an **independent security researcher**.

Your gaming handle, your work profile, the photo app you forgot about: on their own they say little. Linked
together by a reused username, an old email or a bio link, they say who you are. Kenveil maps those links
**for the accounts you prove are yours**, ranks the fixes that close the most paths, and shows your new score
before you change anything.

### 🧰 One license, eleven tools

| | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🕸️ | **Graph** | Maps how your verified accounts, handles, links and public details connect. |
| 🎚️ | **Simulator & Fixes** | Fixes ranked by impact, with your score before you change anything. |
| 🧭 | **Paths** | The chains of public signals that connect your groups, strongest first. |
| ✂️ | **Split** | Decide which parts of your online life stay apart. |
| 🖼️ | **Post** | See what a photo gives away (GPS, camera, QR codes) and save a clean copy, on your PC. |
| 📡 | **Watch** | Scheduled re-scans that tell you when something new becomes public. |
| ♻️ | **Reborn** | Guided removals from people-search sites, with reminders to check they stay gone. |
| 🐤 | **Canary** | A unique email address per site, so you know who leaked or sold yours. |
| 🪞 | **Mirror** | Finds accounts that imitate yours. |
| 🔒 | **Lock** | A calm, step-by-step plan for when your privacy was compromised. |
| 🛰️ | **Relay & Routes** | Your traffic through the Kenveil server over WireGuard, with ready-made profiles. |

**New in 0.2.0:** a **Connect** hub, a browser extension that connects to the app by itself, and an
**Inbox** that reads your own mail in Kenveil, read-only and without trackers.

### 🛡️ How it treats your data

- **Only your own accounts.** Scans start from accounts you verified. There is no field to look up anyone else.
- **Encrypted with your own key.** Accounts, scans and alerts are encrypted with a key unique to your account (AES-256-GCM).
- **Everything written down.** The [Ledger](https://kenveil.com/ledger) lists every piece of data Kenveil keeps: where, why and for how long.
- **EU servers, no trackers.** Servers in Spain. The website loads nothing from other companies and sets no tracking cookies.
- **Nothing hidden on your PC.** Installs for your Windows user only: no admin rights, no Windows service, no kernel driver.
- **Checkable downloads.** Every release is published with its [SHA-256 checksum](https://kenveil.com/download#verify).
- **A warrant canary**, renewed every month: [kenveil.com/transparency](https://kenveil.com/transparency).

What isn't done yet is listed just as plainly on the [trust report](https://kenveil.com/trust#not-yet).
