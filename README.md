## About

Kenveil is built and run by an **independent security researcher**.

Your gaming handle, your work profile, the photo app you forgot about: on their own they say little.
Linked by a reused username, an old email address or a bio link, they say who you are. Kenveil maps
those links for the accounts **you prove are yours**, ranks the fixes that close the most paths, and
shows your new score before you change anything.
