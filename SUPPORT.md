# Getting help with Kenveil

**The fastest way to reach a person:** open a ticket in the [Kenveil Discord](https://discord.gg/2XQBGQDGMf)
and choose the category that fits:

| Category | For |
|---|---|
| Buying a license | Questions before or while you buy |
| Payment or refund | A payment that didn't arrive, went through twice, or a refund |
| Activation problem | A key that won't activate on your PC |
| The app is not working | Errors, crashes, something that looks wrong |
| Something else | Everything else, and security reports (title starting with `SECURITY`) |

Signed in on [kenveil.com/account](https://kenveil.com/account), you can also write to support from your account.

## Before you ask

- **Setup and features:** [kenveil.com/features](https://kenveil.com/features) and the [guides](https://kenveil.com/guides)
- **Is something down?** [kenveil.com/status](https://kenveil.com/status)
- **What changed in a version:** [changelog](https://kenveil.com/changelog)
- **What Kenveil stores about you:** [the Ledger](https://kenveil.com/ledger)

Please never post a full license key, a password or a pairing code in a public channel.
