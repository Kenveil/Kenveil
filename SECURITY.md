# Security policy

Kenveil asks people to trust it with how their accounts connect, so security reports are taken
seriously and answered by a person.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately. Do not open a public issue or post details in a public channel.

1. Join the [Kenveil Discord](https://discord.gg/2XQBGQDGMf) and open a **private ticket**.
2. Choose the category **Something else** and start the title with `SECURITY`.
3. Describe what you found.

The same steps are on [kenveil.com/security](https://kenveil.com/security#disclosure), and in
[security.txt](https://kenveil.com/.well-known/security.txt).

### What to include

- What is affected: a page, an API route, or the app or extension version
- Steps to reproduce it, with a minimal proof of concept
- What someone could do with it

## In scope

- The website and customer account at `kenveil.com`
- The API at `api.kenveil.com`
- The Kenveil app for Windows and the Kenveil browser extension, latest version

## Please don't

- Access, change or delete data that isn't yours. Test with your own account.
- Degrade the service: no denial of service, spam or high-volume scanning.
- Use social engineering, or target staff or other users.
- Publish details before we have had a fair chance to fix the issue.

## Safe harbour

Research done in good faith and within these rules is welcome. We won't take action against you for
it, and we'll work with you to understand and fix the issue.

## Supported versions

Fixes go into the latest release of the app and the extension, published on
[kenveil.com/download](https://kenveil.com/download) with their SHA-256 checksums.
